# Content Strategy

## Goal of every post
1. Get the right founder to remember Trishla.
2. Drive a DM, a profile click, or a portfolio visit.
3. Build authority in AI agents + automation specifically.

## Cadence
- 5 posts per week (Mon-Fri).
- Best time: 9:00 AM IST on weekdays.
- One post per day. Quality > volume.

## 7-day rotation
| Day | Theme | Pillar |
|-----|-------|--------|
| Mon | "Build of the week" — a system I shipped or rebuilt | AI agents |
| Tue | Hot take / contrarian on AI hype vs reality | AI agents |
| Wed | How-to / framework — solve one micro-problem | Web / automation |
| Thu | Behind-the-scenes — failure, lesson, screenshot | Personal |
| Fri | Case study or client outcome — numbers up front | All |

## Post structure (every post)
- **Hook (1-2 lines):** stop the scroll. Number, contradiction, or confession.
- **Body (5-12 lines):** one sentence per line. Tell the story or lay out the framework.
- **CTA (1-2 lines):** ask, invite, or link.
- **Hashtags (3-6):** include `#AIautomation` and at least one of `#LangChain` `#FastAPI` `#NextJS`.

## What NOT to do
- No "what AI can do for your business" generic posts.
- No engagement-bait questions with no payoff.
- No "I asked GPT to do X" filler.
- No more than one hashtag-stuffed line.
- No copying viral templates verbatim.
