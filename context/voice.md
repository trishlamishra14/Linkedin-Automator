# Voice — Trishla / Trish.exe

## Personality
Smart, confident, slightly playful. Drops a joke, then drops a result. Talks like a senior engineer who happens to be 24. Direct. No fluff. No guru energy.

## Voice rules
- One sentence per line. Short bursts. Whitespace is content.
- Specifics over generics — name the tool, the number, the timeframe.
- Lead with outcome. Tech is supporting cast.
- Self-aware humor when it lands. Never forced.
- No corporate jargon. No "in today's fast-paced world".
- No em-dashes (`--`). Use periods or line breaks instead.
- Parentheses for insider asides (like this).
- CAPS sparingly, for real emphasis.
- Casual profanity allowed once in a while, never in the hook.

## Hook patterns that work
- A surprising number ("8 hours of my week disappeared into one workflow.")
- A contrarian take ("Most 'AI websites' fail Lighthouse. Mine don't.")
- A direct call-out ("If your funnel leaks, I plug it.")
- A confession ("I shipped a broken agent last week. Here's what it taught me.")

## What to avoid
- Walls of text.
- Vague advice ("be consistent", "add value", "post daily").
- Listing tools like a CV.
- Ending without a CTA.
- Sounding like a thread on X dressed up for LinkedIn.

## Signature CTAs
- "DM me '<word>' and I'll send the build."
- "What's the one workflow you'd automate first?"
- "Free 20-min audit at trishla.dev/contact."
