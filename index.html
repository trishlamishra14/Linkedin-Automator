How it works

Four agents. One pipeline. Your voice.

Each agent does one job well. The output of one becomes the input to the next. You stay in the loop at every step you choose to be — or you don't, and it just runs.

01

Analyze your voice

Paste a profile URL. Playwright scrapes your bio & recent posts. Claude extracts niche, tone, hook patterns.

02

Watch the world

HackerNews, Reddit, Google News — every morning. Claude scores each item 0-100 against your niche.

03

Draft in your voice

Pick a topic or trend. Claude writes hook + body + CTA matching your brand brief. Add an AI-generated image.

04

Ship on schedule

Review, edit, schedule. Playwright opens a real browser, types the post, hits Publish. Screenshot saved as proof.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A single self-hosted Python service. No SaaS lock-in. No monthly subscription. Your data stays on your machine.

Voice analysis

Scrapes your bio + 12 recent posts. Extracts niche, audience, tone, hook patterns, signature phrases, CTA style. ~47 seconds.

Multi-source trend scout

HackerNews, Reddit (any subreddit), Google News RSS. Free APIs. Daily auto-refresh via APScheduler cron.

Niche relevance scoring live

Claude scores every trend 0-100 against your analyzed brand profile. Only the top matches surface in the dashboard.

A/B variants v0.3

Generate 3 takes on the same topic — different framings, voice-match scores. Pick the best, ship it.

AI-generated images

Replicate Flux Schnell at ~$0.003 per image. Claude crafts the prompt; image attaches to your post automatically.

Cron scheduler

Schedule one post or a recurring cadence. Three modes: dry-run, manual review, fully autonomous.

Engagement Inbox v0.4

Comments & DMs land in an inbox. Claude drafts replies in your voice — you approve and send.

Performance analytics v0.5

Time-of-day heatmap, top posts, engagement-by-topic. The agent learns what's working and weights drafts accordingly.

Self-hosted & private

SQLite on your disk. Your LinkedIn session in your local state.json. No telemetry, no third-party data ever.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Will this get my LinkedIn account flagged?

Risk is low if you stay human-paced. The default mode is manual_review — every post needs your approval, you click Publish at most once a day. LinkedIn's terms of service technically restrict automation, but mainstream tools (Buffer, Taplio, Phantombuster) all operate in this gray area. The realistic line is: post ~daily with human review, you're fine. Crank it to 5×/day fully autonomous and you'll get flagged. Don't do that.

What happens when LinkedIn changes their UI?

The Playwright selectors live in one dict at the top of linkedin_poster.py. When a button moves, it's a 3-line fix. Historically LinkedIn updates the post composer once every 6-9 months. Pull requests welcome.

Do I need to know Python?

To run it, no — clone the repo, copy .env.example to .env, fill in three keys, run python run.py. To customize the brand voice prompts, you edit markdown files in context/. To extend the agents, yes, basic Python helps.

Why not use the LinkedIn API instead of browser automation?

LinkedIn's Marketing API requires multi-week app approval and is restricted in what it allows for personal accounts. Playwright works today on any account. The trade-off is selector maintenance — see above.

Is my LinkedIn login stored anywhere?

Locally in a state.json file Playwright generates after your first successful sign-in. It's git-ignored. Nothing leaves your machine.

Can I post on behalf of clients (agency use)?

v0.2 is single-account. Multi-account support is on the roadmap (Sprint 6). Workaround for now: clone the repo per client, run on separate ports.

How do I deploy this to a server instead of my laptop?

Drop the repo on a $5/mo VPS (Hetzner, Railway, Fly.io). FastAPI runs on uvicorn; Playwright needs Chromium installed. Run python run.py as a systemd service. 30 minutes total.
